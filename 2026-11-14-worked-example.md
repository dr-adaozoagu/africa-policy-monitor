# Signal note — worked example

**African Continental Health Data Governance Framework**
14 November 2026 · Priority: HIGH

## What changed

AU Member States validated the framework at a workshop held at Africa CDC
headquarters in Addis Ababa on 21–22 October 2026. Africa CDC has said it will
finalise the text on the agreed amendments and advance it through AU policy
processes, with consideration anticipated during 2027.

The text is **validated, not adopted**. That distinction is the whole
opportunity: between validation and adoption, a draft is still editable.

## Why it matters to DTH-Lab

It is the cluster's first continental instrument on the lawful, ethical and
sovereign governance of health data, and its architecture is federated —
countries share across borders without surrendering oversight of national
systems. Consent, secondary use, and the treatment of data generated by young
people sit inside provisions of exactly this kind, and they are rarely written
with young people in view.

It touches three of the four workstreams: digital, data and AI governance;
digitally enabled health systems; and digital citizenship for health.

## The next decision point

Here is the complication. The AU Specialised Technical Committee on Health,
Nutrition, Population and Drug Control meets **biennially**, and its 6th Ordinary
Session runs 16–20 November 2026. There is therefore **no ordinary STC-Health in
2027**.

If the framework moves next year, it must travel by another route: an
extraordinary session, the PRC and Executive Council, or Africa CDC's own
governance structures. Monitoring should be set accordingly — watching the
health committee through 2027 would mean watching an empty room.

## Recommended action

1. Establish, through Africa CDC's partner channels, which organ is expected to
   consider the framework and when preparatory documentation is published.
2. Prepare a short youth-centred commentary on the consent and secondary-use
   provisions now, so it is ready when a window opens rather than drafted after.
3. Track the parallel AI Strategy review during 2027 — the two texts will shape
   each other, and the review is a more conventionally open process.

## Confidence

Validation date and status: high, from Africa CDC's own statement. Routing
through policy organs in 2027: **inferred** from the committee's biennial cycle,
not announced. Verify before acting.
