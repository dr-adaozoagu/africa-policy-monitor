# Africa cluster: 2027 decision calendar

The point of a calendar like this is not to list events. It is to show where a
text can still change, and by when. Dates marked *(inferred)* come from an
annual pattern rather than an announcement, and should be confirmed before
anyone plans around them.

## One finding worth stating plainly

**There is no ordinary STC on Health, Nutrition, Population and Drug Control in
2027.** The committee meets biennially and its 6th Ordinary Session runs 16–20
November 2026. So if the Continental Health Data Governance Framework moves
through AU policy organs during 2027, it cannot take the obvious health route.
It would have to go via an extraordinary session, through the PRC and Executive
Council, or through Africa CDC's own governance structures.

Anyone monitoring this cluster by watching the health committee will miss the
year the framework actually moves.
