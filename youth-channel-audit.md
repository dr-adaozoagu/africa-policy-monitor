# Where youth participation is formally provided for — and where it is not

Youth engagement across this cluster is real but almost entirely **event-based**.
Platforms exist. Routes into the drafting of instruments mostly do not. The
pattern below is the strategic case for a liaison function.

| Process | Formal youth channel? | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Africa CDC / CPHIA | Yes — Youth Pre-Conference, scholarships, rapporteurs | Event-based. Recommendations feed into the conference, not into governance |
| AU youth architecture | Yes — Youth Envoy (Vanessa Nakate, appointed 22 Oct 2026), Pan-African Youth Forum, Africa Youth Day | Strong on youth policy as a subject; weak on links to the health and ICT technical committees |
| STC-HNPDC / STC-CICT | No — Member States and experts only; partners attend open segments | Thin |
| Continental Health Data Governance Framework | Member State validation, with partner organisations supporting | Thin. Civil society enters through partners, not directly |
| PHC Digitalisation Framework | Member State validation | Thin |
| ECOWAS | West African Youth Forum on AI (Commission level) | Exists for AI broadly; nothing equivalent at WAHO or the Assembly of Health Ministers |
| WHO Global Strategy on Digital Health 2028–2033 | Open online consultation | Genuinely open, but time-limited — the window closed 1 Sept 2026 |

## What follows from this

The gap is not a shortage of youth platforms. It is that none of the existing
platforms connects to the rooms where health-data and AI instruments are
drafted. Three consequences for how the role should be worked:

1. **Partner routes beat direct routes.** Where frameworks are validated through
   Member States and supporting partner organisations, the realistic path for
   DTH-Lab's evidence runs through those partners, not through a youth platform.
2. **Open consultations are rare and short.** The WHO window was the clearest of
   2026 and it lasted two months. Missing one costs a year or more, which is
   precisely the argument for automated monitoring.
3. **National roadmaps are the accessible layer.** Costed PHC digitalisation and
   CHW roadmaps are drafted at country level, where a Nigerian contributor has
   standing that no one has at continental level.
