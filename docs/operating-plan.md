# Operating plan
## Before appointment
Use this as personal preparation and an interview evidence portfolio. Do not represent yourself as appointed or speak for DTH-Lab. Confirm cohort eligibility, concurrent fellowship arrangements and commitments directly through the application process.

## If appointed: inception (February–July 2027, provisional)
- Agree priority institutions/processes, useful information, escalation thresholds, supervisor check-ins, briefing cadence, approved engagement routes and evidence handling.
- Maintain a policy calendar, stakeholder/mandate map and consultation register.
- Pilot the briefing and opportunity-to-action process; collect supervisor feedback and revise.
- At inception close, record what was tested, what worked and the agreed handover/process for future cohorts.

## Remainder (August 2027–July 2028, provisional)
Continue the agreed system, follow policy processes across annual cycles, contribute to relevant briefs/submissions with credit, document open-session participation and share useful insights via the youth partnerships focal point. Keep check-ins, cohort meetings and annual workshop dates tied to actual invitations.

## Practical allocation within two working days per month
Proposed, to agree: 0.5 day triage/verification; 0.5 day synthesis and concise update; 0.5 day engagement or contribution; 0.5 day check-ins, youth sharing and evidence. Automatic scans run without consuming your working time, but reviewing them does consume it. Additional event time requires prior agreement.

## Triage
Act: verified engagement deadline within 72 hours or supervisor decision needed.
Plan: verified engagement opportunity or material policy transition with a feasible route.
Watch: relevant framework/process without a verified participation route or deadline.
Discard/archive: duplicates, expired calls, generic news with no policy relevance. A validated framework is not automatically an adopted instrument or an open consultation.

## Regional coverage
Core: AU, Africa CDC, ECOWAS and WAHO. Rotate deeper review across the eight AU-recognised REC ecosystems: ECOWAS, EAC, SADC, COMESA, IGAD, ECCAS, UMA and CEN-SAD. WHO AFRO and DTH-Lab provide context. Titles, dates, institutions, document versions, original source URLs and verification dates must travel with every actionable entry. The watchlist is a starting configuration, not a guarantee of full coverage.
